Chapter 10.2 · Negative externality

Gasoline: work the recipe

Buyers and sellers determine the market quantity. Bystanders do not get a vote.

Thumbs up or down before the reveal

Is the socially optimal quantity of pollution zero?

No—not if eliminating it also eliminates valuable activity. The social optimum balances the marginal benefit of gasoline against its full marginal social cost. “Pollution is bad” does not imply “zero at any cost.”

Three facts from Section 10.4

“Public good” does less work than people think

1Government provision does not make it a public good.Postal delivery is rival and excludable.
2A public good need not pass cost-benefit analysis.Classification does not establish that it is worth funding.
3Government funding does not require government production.Finance the fireworks; contract with someone who knows explosives.

Rival + nonexcludable

Private gains. Shared costs. Too much use.

Low wages are not the whole cost calculation

Why don’t all jobs move to the lowest-wage country?

Businesses care about labor cost per unit of output—not wages alone.

Higher wages often travel with higher productivity, better infrastructure, more capital, and specialized skills.

But trade can still raise demand for some workers and reduce it for workers in import-competing sectors.

Section 9.5 · The “or what?” test

A $5 shirt was made in Bangladesh under grim conditions

Your friend says:
“Charge $10 instead. Then the workers will be much better off.”

The moral comparison is not “$2 an hour versus an American wage.” It is “this job—or what?” That does not make bad conditions good. It makes the counterfactual indispensable.

Equilibrium

The point where there is no tendency for change

Quantity demanded=Quantity supplied

Every buyer willing to pay the market price can find a seller, and every seller willing to accept it can find a buyer.

Equilibrium describes where the market comes to rest. It does not certify that the outcome is fair, efficient, or wise.

Shortage: buyers queue and bid against one another Surplus: sellers discount and compete for buyers

A shortage does not mean “expensive.” It means quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied at the current price.

Three questions

  2. 2Which direction?Increase or decrease?

The curve that does not shift still matters because the new equilibrium moves along it.

Elasticity

A measure of responsiveness

InelasticQuantity changes little

Few good alternatives or little flexibility

ElasticQuantity changes a lot

Good alternatives or plenty of flexibility

The arithmetic gives the idea a number. The economic question comes first: how easily can people change their plans?

Demand elasticity

How easy is it for buyers to say “or what?”

More inelastic

Urgent need, few substitutes, broad market, little time to adjust

More elastic

Good substitutes, easy search, narrow market, more time to adjust

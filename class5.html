Cigarette tax · Case 1

The tax is levied on sellers

Which curve moves?
Buyers pay $7 Sellers keep $4 Quantity falls to 13 billion packs.

A tax on sellers raises marginal cost, so the supply curve shifts up by the tax.

Cigarette tax · Case 2

The tax is levied on buyers

Now which curve moves?
Buyers pay $7 Sellers keep $4 Quantity falls to 13 billion packs.

A tax on buyers lowers their marginal benefit, so the demand curve shifts down by the tax.

Chapter 18.5 · Grim Trigger and credible punishment

Cooperation needs a future

1Repeated interactionToday’s move changes tomorrow’s treatment.
2No known final roundA fixed endpoint unravels cooperation backward.
3Observable behaviorYou must know whether the other side defected.
4Credible consequencesThe threatened response must actually be in your interest.

The threat of punishment can sustain cooperation—and if it works, the punishment never needs to occur.
